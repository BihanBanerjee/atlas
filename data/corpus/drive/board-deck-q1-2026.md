---

## 2. Financial detail

Burn increased $14,000 per month over the quarter, split roughly evenly between two engineering hires and infrastructure. Infrastructure is running hotter than modelled — our own load-generation workloads are now a material line item, which is a good problem to have discovered before a customer did.

Gross margin 78%, down two points, same cause.

We remain on plan for the FY26 budget approved in November.

---

## 3. Product

**Shipped this quarter:** results diffing in CI, scheduled test runs, three integrations.

**In progress:** distributed tracing, private beta targeted for April.

Tracing is the largest bet we're making this year. It moves us from "the tool you use before a release" to "the tool that's on during the incident," and it's the difference between a $20k account and a $80k one.

---

## 4. Go-to-market

Self-serve continues to generate the majority of new logos and a minority of new revenue. Enterprise deals close at 6.2x the ACV of self-serve, with a sales cycle of 71 days against 4.

Trial-to-paid conversion is 4.1% and has been flat since October. We believe onboarding friction is the cause and are testing a simplified first-run in April.

**Lost deals this quarter:** three. Two to incumbents bundling into existing observability contracts, one on price.

---

## 5. Team

Eleven people. Two engineering hires closed in Q1.

Dana is currently the only engineering manager, with six direct reports. This is workable now and won't be by mid-year.

---

## 6. Discussion — Series A timing

Current plan has us raising in Q4 2026 with roughly nine months of runway remaining at the point of starting.

**Recommendation from Nadia, for discussion:** begin groundwork in Q2 instead. Build the materials, start the relationships, and be in a position to run a process from a position of choice rather than need.

The counter-argument is distraction. A raise costs the CEO roughly half of three months.

**Resolution:** agreed to start Q2 groundwork. Formal approval to raise deferred to the Q2 meeting.
