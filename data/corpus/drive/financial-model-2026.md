## Assumptions

- Monthly MRR growth 9.5% for the remainder of FY26, below the trailing average of 11.0% as a deliberate conservatism
- Gross churn 0.9% monthly
- Net revenue retention holds at 118%
- Q3 hiring: none. Q4 hiring: Head of Engineering, two engineers, one AE
- Infrastructure at 18% of revenue, down from 22% after the Q2 reduction
- No revenue attributed to unshipped capabilities

## Sensitivities

**If Northwind does not renew:** −$84,000 ARR from October, roughly 7% of forecast exit ARR, and a 0.4 month reduction in runway.

**If the round closes at $8M rather than $12M:** hiring plan reduces from six to three, enterprise work sequences rather than parallelises, and runway is 16 months rather than 24.
