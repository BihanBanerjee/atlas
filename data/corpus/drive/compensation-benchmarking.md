## Conclusion

We were sitting near the 40th percentile across engineering, which explains
two offer declines in Q1 and roughly six weeks lost per attempt.

**Recommendation, adopted May 2026:** move all engineering bands to the 60th
percentile. Cost impact roughly $34,000 annually across current headcount,
against a hiring delay cost we estimated as considerably higher.

Anything above band requires board approval before an offer is issued.
