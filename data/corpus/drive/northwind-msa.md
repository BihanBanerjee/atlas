# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between:** Tensile, Inc. ("Provider")
**And:** Northwind Logistics Ltd. ("Customer")
**Effective Date:** 30 September 2025

---

## 1. Definitions

**"Service"** means the Tensile load testing and performance monitoring platform as described in the applicable Order Form.

**"Named User"** means an individual authorised by Customer to access the Service under Customer's account.

**"Order Form"** means an ordering document executed by both parties referencing this Agreement.

---

## 2. Term

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months, expiring **30 September 2026** (the "Initial Term").

**2.2 Renewal.** This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any continuation beyond the Initial Term requires a new Order Form executed by both parties prior to expiry.

**2.3 Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate on thirty (30) days written notice of a material breach that remains uncured.

---

## 3. Fees

**3.1** Annual fee for the Initial Term: **$84,000 USD**, invoiced annually in advance.

**3.2** The fee covers up to forty (40) Named Users. Usage above that threshold is billed quarterly in arrears at $150 per additional Named User per month.

**3.3** Payment terms net thirty (30) days from invoice date.

---

## 4. Service Levels

**4.1 Availability.** Provider will maintain 99.5% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance.

**4.2 Service credits.** Where monthly availability falls below the commitment:

| Monthly availability | Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0% – 99.5% | 5% of monthly fee |
| 95.0% – 99.0% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Below 95.0% | 25% of monthly fee |

**4.3 Support.** Business hours support, 09:00–18:00 UK time, weekdays. Sev-1 response within four (4) hours.

---

## 5. Data Protection and Security

**5.1** Provider shall process Customer Data solely to provide the Service.

**5.2** Provider shall maintain industry-standard technical and organisational measures, including encryption at rest and in transit.

**5.3** Provider shall notify Customer of any personal data breach within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware.

**5.4** Sub-processors are listed at tensile.dev/subprocessors. Provider will give thirty (30) days notice of additions.

**5.5** Customer Data is hosted in the eu-west-2 region.

---

## 6. Access and Authentication

**6.1** Customer is responsible for managing its Named Users and for the security of their credentials.

**6.2** Provider requires multi-factor authentication on all accounts.

---

## 7. Limitation of Liability

**7.1** Neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim.

**7.2** Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

---

## 8. General

**8.1** Governing law: England and Wales.

**8.2** This Agreement, together with any Order Form, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior representations, whether oral or written.

**8.3** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

---
