Everyone,

Softer month than the last four, and I want to be straight about why.

**Metrics**

- ARR: $940,000 (+9.3% MoM)
- MRR: $78,300
- Net burn: $181,000
- Cash: $1.72M
- Runway: 9 months
- Customers: 56
- Team: 13

**What went well**

Q2 board meeting on 18 June. We approved the Series A raise formally and set a target of $12M.

First conversation with Aster Ventures on 4 June went well — James has spent real time in this category and asked better questions than most. Several other conversations in flight.

Tracing GA work is on schedule for Q3.

**What didn't**

Growth decelerated to 9.3% from an average of about 11.5% over the previous three months. Two things caused it, and neither is a surprise once you look.

First, only one of the two deals that slipped from May actually closed in June. The other is still in security review.

Second, self-serve conversion dipped back to 5.4% after a pricing page change we made mid-month. We've reverted it.

Separately, and more concerning than either: our largest account, Northwind, has enterprise requirements tied to their renewal at the end of September. We are working through what that means. I'd rather flag it now than in the September update.

**Asks**

Introductions to funds that lead at $10-15M. We have good conversations going but I'd like more shots on goal before we're in a position where we need one of them to say yes.
