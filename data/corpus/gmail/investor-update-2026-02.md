Everyone,

February in numbers, then the detail.

**Metrics**

- ARR: $620,000 (up ~11% on January)
- MRR: $51,700
- Net burn: $155,000
- Cash: $2.40M
- Runway: 15 months
- Customers: 38
- Team: 11

**What went well**

Load testing continues to pull its weight — most of the month's new ARR came from teams who found us while trying to stress-test a release and stayed for the monitoring. Two mid-market accounts upgraded off the entry tier without us asking, which is the first time that's happened twice in one month.

Dana's team shipped the results-diffing feature that a handful of customers had been asking for since December. Early signal is good; three accounts have started using it in CI.

**What didn't**

Our self-serve trial-to-paid conversion sat at 4.1%, which is roughly where it's been since October. We've been treating that as acceptable and I no longer think it is. Onboarding is the suspect — people get through signup and then stall before running a meaningful test.

**Asks**

Introductions to platform engineering and SRE leaders at companies over 200 engineers. That's where our best-fit accounts have come from, and cold outbound into that group has been slow going.
