All,

Good month. Numbers first.

**Metrics**

- ARR: $690,000 (+11.3% MoM)
- MRR: $57,500
- Net burn: $162,000
- Cash: $2.24M
- Runway: 14 months
- Customers: 43
- Team: 12

**What went well**

Five net new customers, our best month since launch. Three came through the referral of an existing account, which is a pattern we'd like to lean into deliberately rather than accidentally.

We held the Q1 board meeting on 12 March. The main outcome was agreeing to start Series A groundwork in Q2 rather than waiting until we're closer to needing it — Nadia was firm on not raising from a position of urgency, and she's right.

Distributed tracing went into private beta with four accounts at the end of the month.

**What didn't**

Burn crept up more than planned, mostly infrastructure. Our own load testing workloads are, ironically, a meaningful line item. Dana has a plan to cut that roughly in half next quarter.

We also lost a deal we expected to close — they went with an incumbent bundled into their existing observability contract. That's the second time we've lost on bundling rather than product.

**Asks**

Recommendations for SOC 2 auditors. We're going to need it for enterprise deals and I'd rather pick well than pick fast.
