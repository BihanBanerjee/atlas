Tracing beta is now with eight accounts and the feedback has been better than I expected — two of them have asked when they can put it in front of their own customers, which is not a question you get about a beta.

Net revenue retention for the quarter came in at 118%. Expansion is doing real work for us now rather than being a rounding error.
