Ravi,

You mentioned the pricing page experiment went badly. Some unsolicited thoughts, ignore freely.

The reason leading with annual hurt conversion is almost certainly not that people dislike annual. It's that you put a commitment decision in front of someone who hadn't yet decided the product works. Annual pricing is a great idea at renewal and a terrible one at signup.

The more interesting number in what you sent me is the enterprise floor. $60,000 is what you set when you had no enterprise customers and were guessing. You now have five, you know what they consume in support, and you know what they're willing to sign. Guessing was correct then. It isn't now.

Second thing, less comfortable. You're about to ship a set of enterprise capabilities. There is a strong argument for raising the floor at the same moment, because the story writes itself — new capabilities, new price. If you ship them and then raise the price six months later, you're asking existing customers to pay more for something they already have.

Peter
