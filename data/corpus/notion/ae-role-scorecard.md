# Role Scorecard — Account Executive

**Owner:** Marcus Oyelaran · **Approved:** July 2026 · **Reports to:** Marcus Oyelaran

## Why this role exists

Marcus is carrying every enterprise deal personally, which caps pipeline at
whatever one person can hold. This is the first dedicated commercial hire
beyond the founder-led motion.

## Mission — first twelve months

1. Own the mid-market and enterprise pipeline below $80k ACV
2. Reach $600,000 in closed new ARR, ramping over the first quarter
3. Build a repeatable qualification process that isn't in Marcus's head

## Must have

- Has sold developer tooling to engineering buyers, not general B2B SaaS
- Comfortable in a technical conversation without a solutions engineer present
- Has carried a quota of $500k or more and hit it
- Has worked somewhere the product had gaps and sold honestly anyway

## Compensation

**Approved band: $75,000 base, $150,000 OTE, 50/50 split.**
Quota $600,000 year one. Equity 0.1% to 0.2%.

Band sits at the 60th percentile per the 2026 benchmarking. Anything above
requires Ravi's approval.

## What candidates must be told

Roughly a fifth of current ARR sits in accounts with requirements we cannot
meet today, and a share of enterprise pipeline stalls in security review for
the same reason. Those capabilities are scheduled for Q4.

This goes in the job description, not just the interview. A candidate who
discovers it in month two is a candidate we lose in month four.

## Interview loop

1. Intro with Marcus — 30 min
2. Discovery call roleplay — 60 min
3. Technical conversation with Dana — 45 min
4. Deal review with Ravi — 45 min
5. Two reference calls, at least one a former manager
