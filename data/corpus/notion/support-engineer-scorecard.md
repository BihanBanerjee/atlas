# Role Scorecard — Support Engineer

**Owner:** Dana Whitfield · **Approved:** July 2026

## Why this role exists

Support currently routes to whoever is on call, which means engineers context-switch
out of feature work to answer questions that are usually configuration rather
than defects. Roughly six hours a week of engineering time.

## Mission — first twelve months

1. Own first-line response for all customer issues
2. Reduce engineering time spent on support to under two hours weekly
3. Build a knowledge base from resolved tickets rather than from imagination

## Must have

- Technical support experience at an infrastructure or developer tools company
- Comfortable reading logs and stack traces
- Can write — most of this role is written communication with engineers

## Nice to have

- Scripting ability, any language
- Has built a knowledge base or documentation set from scratch

## Compensation

**Approved band: $55,000 – $65,000 base. Equity 0.03% to 0.06%.**

Band sits at the 58th percentile per the 2026 benchmarking.

## Note on career path

Candidates ask whether this leads into engineering. The honest answer is that
we are fourteen people and boundaries are porous, but no formal path exists.
Do not promise one. Career frameworks are something the Head of Engineering
will build.

## Interview loop

1. Intro with Dana — 30 min
2. Written exercise — respond to three real anonymised tickets
3. Debugging conversation with an engineer — 45 min
4. One reference call
