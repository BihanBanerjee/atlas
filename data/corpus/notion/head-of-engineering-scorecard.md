# Role Scorecard — Head of Engineering

**Owner:** Dana Whitfield · **Approved:** May 2026 · **Reports to:** Dana Whitfield

## Why this role exists

Dana is currently carrying architecture, delivery sequencing, and people management for a team of six, going to ten. Roughly 35% of her week is in one-to-ones and performance conversations, which is time not spent on the parts of the product only she can do.

This role takes delivery and people. Architecture stays with Dana.

## Mission — first twelve months

1. Own quarterly delivery. The roadmap ships on the dates it says, or the dates change before the quarter starts rather than during it.
2. Grow the team from six to ten without a drop in shipping velocity.
3. Establish an on-call rotation that doesn't route everything to Dana.

## Must have

- Has managed engineers through a 2x team growth, ideally at Series A to B stage
- Distributed systems depth sufficient to review design docs credibly
- Has owned a quarterly planning process end to end
- Has hired at least six engineers personally

## Nice to have

- Developer tools or observability background
- Experience with an enterprise readiness push — SSO, audit, compliance
- Has worked with a technical founder and not resented it

## Compensation

**Approved band: $240,000 base, up to 0.8% equity, standard four-year vest with a one-year cliff.**

Band approved by Ravi and Nadia Osei in May. It sits at roughly the 60th percentile for the role at this stage, which is deliberate — we were losing candidates at the 40th percentile earlier this year and lost six weeks per attempt doing it.

Anything above this band needs board approval before an offer goes out.

## Interview loop

1. Intro with Ravi — 45 min
2. Technical depth with Dana — 90 min
3. Delivery and planning case study — 60 min
4. Two peer conversations with senior engineers
5. Reference calls — minimum three, at least two direct reports
