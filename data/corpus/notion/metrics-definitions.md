# Metrics Definitions

How we calculate what we report. If a number appears in a board deck or an
investor update, it is calculated this way.

## Revenue

**MRR** — sum of monthly recurring contract value across active subscriptions
at month end. Annual contracts divided by twelve. Usage overage excluded.

**ARR** — MRR × 12. We do not annualise a partial month.

**Running ARR** — the same calculation applied mid-month for internal weekly
reporting. Not comparable with month-end ARR and never reported externally.

**New MRR** — from customers who were not paying in the prior month.

**Expansion MRR** — increase from existing customers via seats or modules.

**Churned MRR** — from customers who cancelled or downgraded.

## Retention

**Gross retention** — MRR retained from the prior-year cohort, excluding
expansion. Cannot exceed 100%.

**Net revenue retention** — same cohort including expansion. Measured annually
on a trailing twelve-month basis, not monthly.

## Cash

**Net burn** — cash out minus cash in, monthly. Not the same as operating loss;
timing differences on annual invoices move it around.

**Runway** — closing cash divided by trailing three-month average net burn.
Rounded down.

## Customers

**Customer** — one paying account. A company with two separate accounts counts
as two. This has not yet mattered but it will.

**Active user** — signed in at least once in the trailing thirty days.

## A note on consistency

The reason this page exists is that in January we reported two different ARR
figures in the same week because two people calculated it differently. If a
definition here is wrong, change it here first and then change the reporting.
