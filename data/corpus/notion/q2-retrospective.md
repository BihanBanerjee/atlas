# Q2 2026 Retrospective

Whole-team retro, 29 June.

## What worked

**Onboarding rebuild.** Smallest piece of work in the quarter, largest measurable
effect — trial-to-paid from 4.1% to 6.3%. Two days of work.

**Infrastructure reduction.** Delivered ahead of plan, gross margin recovered
two points.

**Compensation band change.** Closed a role in three weeks that had been open
for two months. Painful to admit we were the problem; obvious in hindsight.

## What didn't

**The pricing experiment.** Ran without an agreed success metric or a stop
condition, so it took a fortnight to conclude something we could have called in
four days. Action: every experiment gets a metric and a stop date before it
starts.

**Tracing slipped out of the quarter.** Same sampling problem we underestimated
in Q1. Action: when an estimate touches a component that has surprised us
before, double it and say why.

**Enterprise requirements are now blocking revenue and nothing is scheduled.**
Identity came up in the Q1 retrospective as unscoped. It came up again in two
stalled deals this quarter. It is still unscoped.

This is the third quarter in a row it has been raised and not planned. The
reason each time has been capacity, and the reason is real, but noting that a
real reason repeated three times starts to look like a decision rather than a
constraint.

## Actions

- [ ] Every experiment gets a metric and a stop date — Dana
- [ ] Estimates touching known-surprising components get doubled — Dana
- [ ] Identity scoped and sized before Q3 planning closes — Dana
- [ ] ARR at risk from enterprise gaps quantified for the board — Ravi
